# Research: Gian Clerici

**Source:** Voice notes shared by Santiago, 2026-10-30. Gian is identified as Sales Lead at HM Cargo, Buenos Aires, specializing in air logistics for human and animal healthcare. He also described experience in commercial pricing.

**Context:** His examples include requesting quotes from providers. Our demo handles inbound calls from carriers seeking loads. Apply the operational insights below, not the outbound-call process. This feedback does not override the challenge requirements.

## Relevant insights

- **Track relationships, not just prices.** In Apps, use Twin records to show each carrier's calls, loads offered, confirmed bookings and reasons for no agreement. This helps operations identify recurring mismatches and follow up. A low conversion rate alone does not establish poor carrier performance.
- **Conditions matter.** Communicate relevant TMS notes, such as loading/unloading time allowances or waiting charges. Flag unclear conditions for the simulated senior rep. Do not invent terms or negotiate additional charges outside the defined policy.
- **Keep a written record.** Preserve the load, agreed rate, conditions and outcome in Twin. Distinguish a pending agreement from a TMS-confirmed booking.

## Demo scope

Prioritize a carrier history and reasons-for-no-agreement view using the existing Twin and Apps architecture. This is a proposed enhancement, not an implemented feature.

Separate internal/external confirmation emails are a later option. Do not add insurance quoting, extra-charge pricing or outbound carrier prospecting from this interview.

Gian did not resolve OTP frequency, booking timing or the TMS rate-field mapping.
