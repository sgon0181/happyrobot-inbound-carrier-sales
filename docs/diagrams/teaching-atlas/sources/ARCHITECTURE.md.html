Eligible only if `allowedToOperate` is `Y`, `commonAuthorityStatus` or `contractAuthorityStatus` is `A`, `statusCode` is `A`, `oosDate` is present and null, and any other out-of-service field says no. `allowedToOperate` alone is not enough, because inactive carriers still showed `Y`. The ledger of checked carriers is in [docs/test-carriers.md](docs/test-carriers.md).3332. **Issue.** The service generates a 6-digit code, stores a keyed hash on the call row with a 5-minute expiry, then sends the email itself over Gmail SMTP. Load search and negotiation342343**Search** normalizes lane and equipment, then requires explicit `OPEN`, a future pickup and no active reservation. `LOAD_QUERY` also returns `PENDING` loads, so returned matches are checked locally. Loads on the [blocked list](service/policy/blocked-loads-v1.json) are never offered. Results are ranked by pickup time, and at most three are offered, one at a time. The agent reads notes as written. When nothing matches, the search is recorded on the call.344345**Rate assumption (D-10).** `MAX_BUY` from `LOAD_GET` is provisionally `max_rate`. Luke did not confirm this mapping or its currency. A load without a usable `MAX_BUY` goes to `pricing_review_required`: no offers, no reservation (D-11).346347**Pricing policy (D-4).** A versioned file, [`pricing-v1`](service/policy/pricing-v1.json), turns each load's posted rate and ceiling into three numbers, all rounded down to 25:348349- **Working cap:** the ceiling minus a load-specific gap of 2 to 6%. It is the highest number we say and the highest ask we accept.350- **Opening offer:** the posted rate minus 5% when that leaves room below the cap, otherwise a load-specific spread of 8 to 15% below the cap.351- **Round targets:** 35%, 65% and 100% of the way from opening to cap.352353Both draws use an HMAC of the policy version, load ID and purpose under a server secret. The same load gives the same numbers on every call, so calling back does not redraw the price. The secret prevents exact recomputation, but the public policy band and observed offers still constrain possible ceilings. These percentages are demo choices the brokerage would own.354355**Rounds.** Each counter advances to the next target. An ask at or below the target is accepted at the carrier's number. Round 3 is the final offer at the cap, and anything but acceptance ends the call as `negotiation_failed`, with no transfer. Rounds count across loads (INV-4).356357**What this protects, and what it costs.** A fixed percentage could be inverted to find the ceiling; the keyed gap removes that formula. Some leakage is unavoidable, because the cap creates an accept/reject boundary that probing can narrow, so confidentiality is a tested property, not a guarantee. A carrier whose last ask falls between cap and ceiling is turned away, measured as *deals lost to the cap*.358359---360361## 10. Reservation and Confirm362363**On the call.** When a carrier accepts, `negotiate` re-reads the load, then inserts the booking row. If that insert loses, the load has gone and the agent says so. Nothing has been sent, so a dropped call, a misheard yes or a failed write leaves at worst a reservation for the rep to review. A reservation does not expire, so it holds the load until a rep acts.364365**Reservation context (R4 local candidate).** The fresh detail read used to price an acceptance also supplies an explicit rep-view allowlist. Its lane, dates, equipment, cargo and posted rate are stored on the booking row and its reserved event; the ceiling is excluded. Reusing a released row preserves the previous snapshot in its event. Old rows have no reconstructed context. Reliability and capacity450451- **Timeouts.** A timeout does not tell us whether a request landed. For reads we retry. For `LOAD_BOOK` we record `UNKNOWN` and stop.452- **Voice deadline.** Every voice request stops at 12 seconds and returns `temporarily_unavailable`. That is safe because no voice request can send a booking. An interrupted code email counts against the send limit and is not retried.453- **Confirm runs to completion.** It has no voice deadline, and a dashboard disconnecting does not cancel it.454- **Twin rate limit.** All statements pass through one pacer at 4 per second, retrying only on HTTP 429. A timed-out write may still have landed, so the next read decides. Conditional writes make that safe.455- **One instance.** One service instance for simplicity. The version check protects against restarts and deployment overlap, without process-local reservation locks.456- **No background jobs.** Stale calls and stuck bookings are detected when read, and resolved by a rep. Saved row state survives restarts. An in-flight email or booking can have an uncertain outcome; a booking receipt not yet saved in Twin can be lost from process memory. Recovery depends on retained logs or the rep receipt, and no automatic resend is allowed.457- **Capacity.** Unproven. Earlier statement-count estimates were not a throughput benchmark and omit pacing, retries, concurrent tools and latency. The single-instance Twin pacer is a constraint, not a measured call rate.
