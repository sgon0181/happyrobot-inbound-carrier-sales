Authority and OTP are different checks5758### Established guidance5960FMCSA distinguishes an active USDOT number from operating authority, including the particular operations an entity is authorized to perform. A broker-of-property authorization is not the same as motor-carrier-of-property authorization. Do not reduce eligibility to `USDOT status == ACTIVE`.[^1_7][^1_8]6162FMCSA’s fraud guidance, published April 7, 2025, recommends checking carrier contact numbers against SAFER and calling the listed number when the supplied number differs. It also warns that documents-including insurance certificates-can be fraudulent. Consequently, a valid MC number does not establish that the caller represents that carrier.[^1_9]6364### Recommended POC6566Have `verify_carrier` return structured evidence:6768```text69carrier_id70legal_name71usdot_number72mc_number73entity_type74operating_authority75out_of_service_status76eligibility: eligible | ineligible | review_required77reason_codes78source79checked_at80```8182Limit the demo’s eligibility policy to a stated operating scope-for example, interstate, for-hire general-property carriers. Do not present those rules as universal freight requirements.8384For OTP:8586- Resolve the destination from a trusted carrier-contact record; do not let the caller supply a replacement number and immediately authenticate against it.87- Bind the challenge to the call and carrier identity.88- Enforce expiration, single use, attempt limits, resend limits, and invalidation of superseded challenges.89- Make successful verification a backend prerequisite for both load search and load-detail retrieval.90- Treat changing the claimed carrier as invalidating verification.9192The business purpose is to prevent someone with a copied MC number from accessing the brokerage’s load inventory.9394### Challenge to your assumption9596OTP proves access to a destination-not employment, dispatch authority, or entitlement to haul a particular shipment.
