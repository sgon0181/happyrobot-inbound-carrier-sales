Remember the lowest valid ask1415The current service does not retain a negotiated running minimum as policy. It accepts an ask at or below the current offer or next target, and can otherwise increase its offer toward a fresh cap. Example: opening 1700, next target 1900, first ask 2000 produces a 1900 counter. A later higher ask can move the next target above 2000. If the caller then accepts that higher counter, the service can pay above the earlier ask while still obeying every current ceiling/round rule. This is a possible commercial inefficiency, not a demonstrated violation of the PDF.1617A number is not enough to authorize an agreement. Before adding memory, the owner must decide:1819- Valid ask: one explicit positive whole amount, passed through negotiate for a verified call and a particular MC, load and negotiation episode. An MC change invalidates that carrier's remembered quote while preserving the call-wide round budget. Vague ranges, guesses, prices for other loads and invalid money do not qualify.20- Correction: "I meant 2200" should replace the mistaken ask, rather than preserving it as a binding minimum. Withdrawal, misunderstanding or "that price was for another lane" also needs explicit treatment.21- Shipment changes: pickup, delivery, equipment, dimensions, commodity or conditions can invalidate an old ask. The TMS supplies no stable shipment revision ID. Define what constitutes changed terms before using history across fresh reads.22- Authority: the remembered ask should bound a new offer only when valid and compatible with fresh terms. It must not silently accept an old amount after the caller raised or withdrew it. Ask for fresh agreement where needed.23- Fresh bounds: if a changed opening/cap no longer fits the proposed remembered bound, the policy must decide what to say. Do not bypass the current fresh-term checks or conceal a changed deal by clamping numbers.2425If approved, keep the decision in core/negotiation.py with explicit inputs, and save any remembered ask/correction alongside the call event inside CallRows.step. Preserve the three-round budget across loads and MC changes, same-ask repeat behavior, one accepted agreement and booking claim recovery. Avoid an extra table, hidden model memory or a second acceptance path.2627Verify with examples of higher/lower asks, correction, withdrawal, unchanged/changed terms, duplicates, stale writers, fresh lower cap, exhausted rounds and locked agreement. Add conversation checks for the necessary clarification. Compare the new policy with the existing one on reproducible inputs before changing the policy version. Do not claim savings from generated examples or confuse a quoted ask with consent.2829## B. Notify the rep in Slack3031The value is an alert that leads to a decision, not another booking interface. The first scope should stay at a new reservation awaiting review and an unknown submission requiring investigation. Exactly-once notification delivery is not established by a row flag.3435Alerts only on saved UNKNOWN transitions do not cover stuck SUBMITTING rows or receipt_not_saved outcomes until reconciliation records UNKNOWN. Do not add that machinery without a concrete response-time need.
