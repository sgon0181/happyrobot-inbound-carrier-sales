# Prospect summary, ready for the broker

Subject: Carrier desk pilot, from incoming call to a rep-reviewed rate

Hi Carrier Sales team,

A carrier can call the HappyRobot web desk, give an MC number, verify by emailed code, hear matching freight and negotiate a rate. The service checks active authority and enforces your pricing policy. At agreement it records the load and rate in Twin and hands the decision to a senior rep. The rep can see the originating call, its price history and any TMS receipt in the App.

In 49 recorded development calls, 36 passed the email check and 19 of those produced a reservation. The median agreement was $50 above our opening offer. Most agreements came from the same carrier, so I would use these results to choose pilot questions rather than forecast conversion or margin. On the separate call where a rep submitted to the sandbox TMS, we received a reference and a PENDING status. The desk kept that booking pending.

The proposal adds checks for shipment changes between pitch and approval, a desk assignment for each rep who can submit, and separate queues for uncertain sends and freight requiring manual review. The updated voice workflow and App are awaiting promotion. The attached walkthrough distinguishes the recorded call, the historical TMS response and the disposable approval exercise.

For a supervised shift I suggest enrolling a small roster of trusted carrier contacts and agreeing which operators may submit. We'll compare matched shifts on missed calls, time to first offer, verified calls producing reservations, and the number of requests left unresolved. We'll review sensitive and failed calls together before increasing volume. What peak arrival rate and wait time should we test against on your busiest shift?
